# Hero asset audit

Cutouts for the homepage cinematic hero. Generated by
`scripts/build-hero-cutouts.py`, measurements in `scripts/_hero_cutout_report.json`.

## Method

Masking only. The original photograph's pixels are preserved; nothing is
redrawn, repainted or generated.

1. Letterboxing auto-trimmed (the sources are 900×1200 JPEGs with large black
   padding around a landscape photo).
2. Optional sub-crop to isolate one subject where the source shows several
   colourways.
3. Background removed with `rembg` / u2net — the same technique the repository
   already used for the previous hero cutout.
4. Reduced to the single largest solid component (drops stray fragments).
5. Trimmed to the alpha bounding box with 16px padding.

Only `toros-jellybean` was resampled (2× Lanczos), because its source region is
far smaller than the rest. No sharpening, denoising or colour correction was
applied — none was needed, and each is a chance to alter the product.

**Not used:** `assets/product-screenshots/cleaned-png/` contains files named
`ChatGPT Image …`. These are AI-produced extractions, and the brief forbids
AI-redrawn knives, so they were deliberately ignored in favour of masking the
real photographs.

## Chosen subjects

- **`toros-jellybean`** — source shows three colourways. The **teal/blue** knife
  was isolated. A first pass at a wider crop pulled in a sliver of the green
  knife beside it; the crop was tightened until the subject was clean.
- **`sakra-bear-claw-neck-knives`** — source shows five colourways. The
  **front pearl/white-handled** knife was isolated.
- **`bos-stag-golden-horn`, `misty-rebar-shank`, `gur-tuva`, `gur-tombik`** —
  the knife rests on its leather sheath and the two touch, so they mask as one
  merged subject. Kept as-is: the sheath ships with the knife, and cutting
  between them would mean inventing an edge that isn't in the photograph.
- **`misty-stubby-giraffe`** — single knife, nothing to choose.

## Weaknesses and recommended reshoots

### `toros-jellybean` — reshoot recommended (highest priority)
Native subject region is only ~136×433 before the 2× resample; it is the
smallest source of the seven and the least able to hold hero scale. Max credible
display: roughly 300px wide. **Reshoot:** one knife alone, isolated, shot
vertically at full resolution.

### `sakra-bear-claw-neck-knives` — reshoot recommended
361×391 usable, cropped out of a five-knife group shot, so the subject occupies
a small slice of the frame. Depth of field from the group shot also softens it.
Max credible display: roughly 380px wide. **Reshoot:** the single sellable
variant on its own.

### `gur-tuva` — usable, but the softest mask of the set
76,359 partially-transparent edge pixels, roughly seven times any other product.
Cause: the dark wooden handle sits against a dark background, so u2net had low
contrast to work with and produced a feathered boundary rather than a crisp one.
It reads correctly on the hero's dark ground — where the softness is invisible —
but would show as a halo on a light background. **Do not reuse this cutout on a
light surface without re-masking.** See also the disputed handle material in
`docs/hero-product-audit.md`.

### General
All seven sources are 900×1200 exports with baked-in wooden backgrounds and
letterboxing. Isolated product shots on a plain sweep would remove the masking
step entirely and materially improve every cutout.

## Swapping in a better asset

Drop a replacement PNG at `public/images/hero/knives/{slug}.png` and update
`cutoutWidth` / `cutoutHeight` in `components/home/hero/hero-knives.ts`. The
stage sizes from the intrinsic dimensions, so no layout changes are needed.
Set `assetStatus: "ready"` and clear `assetNote` once a reshoot lands.
