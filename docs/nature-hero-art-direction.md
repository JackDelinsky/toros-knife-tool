# Nature hero art direction

What each of the seven hero environments is, what it is made of today, and
what it should become when purpose-shot photography exists.

## The approach, and its one significant deviation

The brief asks for seven wide wilderness landscapes: ridgelines, a creek, an
open highland. That is not what shipped, and the reason is worth stating
plainly.

There is no image generation available in this repository, and rendering
wilderness procedurally produced an illustration rather than a photograph —
the first attempt is in the history and it was not good enough to ship. What
*is* available is the makers' own photography: every knife was shot outdoors
on a real weathered stump in real light, with real depth of field.

So the scenes are built from those photographs instead. Each one is the
largest rectangle of its knife's own photograph that contains no knife,
extended, defocused and graded into five layers. The result is a
product-scale outdoor scene rather than a landscape: a real log with real
grain, in the light it was really photographed in.

Two consequences, both deliberate:

- **No ridgelines, no creek, no highland.** Nothing photographed one. The
  scene bible's concepts are recorded below as the shot list for when they
  can be photographed, not faked in the meantime.
- **The seven are separated by grade, not geography.** The sources share one
  grey afternoon on one stump, so season and time of day are carried by
  colour temperature, exposure and one accent each. With products and text
  hidden the seven are clearly different conditions; they are not yet
  clearly different places.

## Composition, shared

- **Surface line.** Every surface plate is cropped to begin at its own top
  edge, and the hero measures where the active knife's feet actually land
  and places the line there. That is why the knife meets the surface at
  360px and at 1920px without a table of per-breakpoint offsets.
- **Desktop.** Copy left, price and actions right, knife centre on the
  surface, neighbours visible in the same environment at reduced scale.
- **Mobile.** Knife and surface on top, copy and actions below on the
  shaded band. Layers are re-anchored rather than centre-cropped.
- **Text safe zones.** The outer thirds carry a gradient; the middle keeps
  its full brightness. Titles measure 10-13:1 against their backdrop and
  prices 4.6-6.6:1, without dimming the scene.

## Rules that hold for every scene

- No more than two visible ambient motions plus one micro-detail.
- Cycles run 12-20s, unsynchronised, transform and opacity only.
- Ambient motion starts when the knife arrives and restarts on revisit; it
  slows to roughly a third while the knife is being handled and stops
  entirely when the page is hidden or the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- The foreground may cross the lowest edge of the product while it rests,
  and clears completely the moment it is picked up.
- Nothing in a plate is a claim about a real location.
